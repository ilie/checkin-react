Checkin on desktop: the Home screen

The phone's greeting, live clock, today's status and this week's hours, laid out for a 1280×800 browser window and styled after macOS 26. Each mockup is drawn at full size and scaled to fit. The figures are the e2e test data: Walter clocked in at 09:05 on Wednesday 4 November.

From Apple's macOS guidelines

  • macOS type scale: Large Title 26/32 for the greeting, Title 3 15/20 for card titles, Body 13/16.
  • Liquid Glass only on the functional layer: the sidebar or toolbar. The cards stay solid.
  • The sidebar icons use the accent colour, and a button shows or hides the sidebar.
  • The week chart starts at zero, with ticks at 0, 4 and 8 hours, a summary title, and the same figures as text in the rows below it.

Carried over from the phone

  • The same palette, accent navy (violet in the dark), round button and wording.
  • The missing check-out warning, the "Last check-out" line, and This week against the contract.
  • No new API calls: the chart and rows come from this week's check-ins, which Home already loads on phones.
